How to Use GMilli AI Hybrid
  1. Connect your Deriv API token or use Paper Trading mode.
  2. Select a market (Volatility 100 recommended).
  3. Watch the AI Prediction Engine — it shows direction & confidence %.
  4. Click HIGHER or LOWER based on the prediction.
  5. Or click HIGHER & LOWER to hedge both directions simultaneously.
  6. Enable Auto Trading to let the AI execute trades automatically.
  7. Use HML mode for 2-strike hedging, or All 4 for all 4 simultaneous strikes.
  8. Monitor your win rate, P&L, and let the AI learn & adapt over time.
